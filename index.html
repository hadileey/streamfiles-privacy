Privacy Policy

Updated: August 19, 2026

StreamFiles ("we", "us", or "our") is a Telegram-based file streaming service that allows users to process and stream files through a Telegram bot and Telegram Mini App.

1. Information We Collect

We receive basic information provided by Telegram, including your user ID, username, first name, and language settings made available to the bot.

2. Files You Send

The bot processes files to provide streaming functionality. We store technical data including file names, sizes, Telegram file identifiers, and processing status.

Users must only share content they have the legal right to distribute.

3. Usage Information

We collect basic usage data such as streaming requests, timestamps, and technical metrics required to secure and operate the service.

4. Sponsored Messages

StreamFiles may send promotional content or links via the bot from advertisers or administrators.

5. Third-Party Services

We use third-party infrastructure for hosting, databases, and security. These partners process information only as necessary to provide these services.

6. Data Retention

Information is retained only as long as necessary to operate the service and comply with legal obligations.

7. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, though no online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Choices

You may stop using the service at any time. Contact us via the bot for any data-related inquiries.

9. Children's Privacy

StreamFiles is not designed to collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to Policy

Updates will be reflected on this page as the service evolves.

11. Contact

Contact the administrator via the official StreamFiles Telegram bot.
