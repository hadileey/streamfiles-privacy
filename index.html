Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

StreamFiles ("we", "us", or "our") is a Telegram-based file streaming service that allows users to process and stream files through a Telegram bot and Telegram Mini App.

1. Information We Collect

When you use StreamFiles, we may receive basic information provided by Telegram, such as your Telegram user ID, username, first name, and language or other information made available to the bot.

2. Files You Send

When you send a file to StreamFiles, the bot may process the file to provide streaming functionality. We may store technical information about the file, such as its name, size, Telegram file identifier, and processing status.

Users should only upload or share content that they have the legal right to use and distribute.

3. Usage Information

We may collect basic usage information such as streaming requests, timestamps, and technical information needed to operate, secure, and improve the service.

4. Sponsored Messages

StreamFiles may send sponsored or promotional messages to users who have started the bot. These messages may contain promotional content and links provided by advertisers or administrators.

5. Third-Party Services

StreamFiles may use third-party services for hosting, databases, storage, analytics, security, and other technical infrastructure. These services may process information as necessary to provide their respective services.

6. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, and resolve technical issues.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information handled by StreamFiles. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Choices

You may stop using StreamFiles at any time. You may also contact us regarding questions about information associated with your use of the service.

9. Children's Privacy

StreamFiles is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as StreamFiles develops. Any significant changes will be reflected on this page.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact the StreamFiles administrator through the official StreamFiles Telegram bot.
